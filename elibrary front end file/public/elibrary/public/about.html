About SUB-LGRRC

What is LGRRC?

The Local Governance Resource Center (LGRRC) is a knowledge management facility established by the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG). It serves as a central hub for governance-related information, learning materials, policy references, and capacity development resources intended for Local Government Units (LGUs).

LGRRC aims to strengthen local governance by providing access to research-based materials, training modules, best practices documentation, and development frameworks that support transparency, accountability, and effective public service delivery.

The Sub-LGRRC functions as an extension of the regional and provincial governance support system, ensuring that LGUs and stakeholders have accessible, updated, and relevant information to enhance institutional capacity and promote sustainable development.

Our Mission

To empower local government units through knowledge sharing, capacity-building initiatives, and access to comprehensive governance resources.

Our Vision

A responsive and innovative knowledge center that supports excellence in local governance.
